Visa tips

Plan well. Apply right. Travel bright.

Eight habits behind a strong Australia visit visa application — and good practice for any destination.

  • Show a clear purpose for your visit — tourism, family visit, business.
  • Provide strong financial evidence.
  • Show your ties to Bangladesh — job, business, family, assets.
  • Maintain a good travel history.
  • Submit genuine and consistent documents.
  • Be honest in your application.
  • Plan your travel dates realistically.
  • Seek professional guidance to avoid mistakes.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Straight answers to what families ask us most. Something else on your mind?

Ask us on WhatsApp
01Which countries do you handle visit visas for?

Australia, New Zealand, Canada, the United Kingdom, Japan, South Korea and the Schengen area of Europe — including France, Italy, the Netherlands, Germany and Spain. Planning somewhere else? Ask us; we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

02Can you help my child study at a school abroad?

Yes. We guide school selection and admission in New Zealand and Canada, the student visa or study permit, and the guardian visa for the parent who will accompany your child. IEIC is the authorised education agent in Bangladesh for Greenpark School in Tauranga, New Zealand.

03What documents will I need?

It depends on the country and the purpose of your trip, so we never hand out a generic list. After your profile assessment you receive a personalised checklist — typically covering your passport, financial evidence, proof of your job or business, family ties and travel plan — and we review every document before it is submitted.

04How long does a visa take?

Processing time is set by each embassy or immigration authority and changes with the season, so nobody can promise a date. We give you a realistic timeline for your destination and help you apply early enough that your travel dates are comfortable.

05Do you guarantee a visa?

No honest consultant can. The decision always belongs to the embassy or immigration authority. What we do is make sure your application is clear, genuine, consistent and well documented — which is what gives it the strongest chance.

06My visa was refused before. Can I apply again?

Often, yes. Bring the refusal letter and we will go through the reasons with you, tell you frankly whether a new application makes sense, and help you address what was missing the first time.

07Do you also book flights and hotels?

Yes — air tickets and hotel bookings in your destination country, planned so that your itinerary and your visa application always agree.

08How do I get started, and what does it cost?

Message us on WhatsApp or call 01637-527956, or visit our office at Level-4, House-12, Road-13, Sector-1, Uttara, Dhaka. We start with a profile assessment, explain your options, and give you a clear quote for our service before you commit. Embassy and visa-centre fees are paid separately.
